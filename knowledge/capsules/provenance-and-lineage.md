# Dataset Provenance & Lineage

Represent lineage as a graph of immutable source objects, document chunks, transformations, derived records, dataset versions, and export artifacts.

A record's surface similarity to authentic data is not evidence of authenticity. Source verification, origin documentation, and license evidence determine provenance status.

Persist origin references, checksums, timestamps, transformation versions, AI invocation identifiers, and review decisions. Derivation and duplication must not silently inflate the count of independent authentic observations.
