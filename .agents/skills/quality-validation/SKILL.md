# Quality Validation
Use when implementing quality reports, review workflows, drift tests, repair, or training readiness.

Separate deterministic criteria (type/schema, uniqueness, constraints, math, references) from probabilistic criteria (plausibility, diversity, prompt compliance).
Measure duplicate rate, distributions, source overlap, leakage risk, provenance coverage, coverage gaps, and downstream validation where available.
Never treat a model judge score as objective proof. Provide methodologies and sample sizes.
Limit repair attempts, preserve original and repaired versions, validate every repair, quarantine unresolved records, and prevent denominator manipulation.
Evaluate composition before and after repair; a replacement generated by Meta may change classification.
Provide clear pass/fail explanations and honest unavailable measurements.
Test deliberately malformed records, contradiction cases, repeated repairs, adversarial content, and export blocking.
